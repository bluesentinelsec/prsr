# Code of Conduct

This is a technical project. Act like an adult.

- Be respectful. Disagreement is expected; personal attacks, harassment, and spam are not.
- Focus on the work: code, design, bugs, documentation, and whether a change is correct.
- Be professional. Argue ideas, not people. Write as if the other person is competent and acting in good faith until they prove otherwise.
- Maintainers may reject a contribution, close a discussion, or block someone who makes the project worse to work on.

If you need to report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer privately (GitHub or email). Do not turn it into a public pile-on.
